Nonfiction Vocabulary

Unit 3-11 · Explain each word in your own English.

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

tusk

nounWhat is a tusk?Think first

Book meaning

A large tooth that sticks out of an animal's mouth.

In your own words

A tusk is a long tooth outside an animal's mouth.

Example

The elephant used its tusk to dig in the dirt.

부모 힌트: 코끼리 이빨 그림을 가리키며 “일반 이빨과 어디가 다를까?” 하고 물어보세요.

unfortunate

adjectiveWas it lucky or unlucky?Think first

Book meaning

Unlucky or sad.

In your own words

It means something sad or bad happened.

Example

It was unfortunate that rain canceled our picnic.

부모 힌트: 비 때문에 소풍을 못 간 장면을 떠올리고 “운이 좋았을까, 안 좋았을까?”라고 물어보세요.

native

adjectiveWhere is it originally from?Think first

Book meaning

Originally from a place.

In your own words

A native animal first lived in that place.

Example

Koalas are native to Australia.

부모 힌트: “이 동물이 원래 어디에서 살았을까?”라고 물어보세요. 사람이나 식물에도 쓸 수 있어요.

pick

verbWhat do you do when you pick?Think first

Book meaning

To select, choose, or say you want something from a place.

In your own words

To pick means to choose the one you want.

Example

I picked a blue shirt from the closet.

부모 힌트: 옷장에서 하나를 고르는 그림을 가리키며 “Which one did you pick?”이라고 물어보세요.

root

nounWhich part grows underground?Think first

Book meaning

The part of a plant that grows underground.

In your own words

A root is the part of a plant under the ground.

Example

The plant's roots spread deep under the soil.

부모 힌트: 식물의 뿌리가 흙 아래에 있고 물을 빨아들인다는 점을 그림이나 화분으로 보여 주세요.

crack

verbWhat happens when something cracks?Think first

Book meaning

To make a small break in something.

In your own words

To crack means to make a small break.

Example

I cracked the egg into a bowl.

부모 힌트: 달걀 껍데기에 금을 내는 동작을 보여 주세요. “완전히 산산조각 난 걸까, 작은 틈이 생긴 걸까?”

nibble

verbBig bites or tiny bites?Think first

Book meaning

To take small bites from something.

In your own words

To nibble means to eat with tiny bites.

Example

The rabbit nibbled a carrot.

부모 힌트: 한입에 크게 베어 먹는 것과 조금씩 베어 먹는 것을 비교해 보세요.

illegal

adjectiveIs it against the law?Think first

Book meaning

Against the law.

In your own words

Illegal means the law says you must not do it.

Example

It is illegal to take things from a store without paying.

부모 힌트: 규칙과 법의 차이를 간단히 설명해 주세요. “법으로 허용되지 않는 일”이라는 핵심을 잡으면 돼요.

rescue

verbWho helps someone out of danger?Think first

Book meaning

To save someone from something.

In your own words

To rescue means to save someone who is in danger.

Example

The lifeguard rescued the swimmer from the water.

부모 힌트: 위험한 상황에 빠진 사람을 안전하게 도와주는 장면을 떠올리게 해 주세요.

under arrest

phraseWhat have the police done?Think first

Book meaning

When the police take a person to jail.

In your own words

It means the police caught the person and took them to jail.

Example

The news said the man was under arrest.

부모 힌트: 경찰이 법을 어겼다고 의심되는 사람을 공식적으로 붙잡은 상태라고 설명해 주세요. 유죄가 확정됐다는 뜻과는 달라요.
