Nonfiction Vocabulary

Week 5 (Week 6 Test) · Vocabulary Cards · Explain each word in your own English.

홈으로

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

take a look

idiom

Meaning

to look at something closely

In simple words

To take a look means to look closely and carefully at something interesting.

Example

Let's take a look at that painting together.

살펴보다, 한번 보다 : 미술관의 멋진 그림이나 흥미로운 것을 가까이 다가가 자세히 들여다보는 행동입니다.

brave

adjective

Meaning

being strong and not afraid

In simple words

Brave means having courage and standing strong without being scared or running away.

Example

The girl was very brave and wasn't scared of the wolf.

용감한 : 무섭거나 어려운 상황 앞에서도 겁먹지 않고 당당하고 씩씩하게 맞서는 모습입니다.

greedy

adjective

Meaning

wanting too many things

In simple words

Greedy describes someone who wants to keep all the treats and toys for themselves without sharing.

Example

Annie was not greedy. Even though she was hungry, she shared her snacks with her friends.

욕심 많은, 탐욕스러운 : 친구들과 나누지 않고 혼자서만 맛있는 도넛이나 과자를 전부 차지하려는 모습입니다.

believe

verb

Meaning

to see something as true

In simple words

To believe means to feel sure in your heart that something is real and true.

Example

I don't believe in aliens, because I've not seen one!

믿다 : 어떤 사실이나 존재가 진짜라고 마음속으로 확신하는 것입니다.

radish

noun

Meaning

a root vegetable

In simple words

A radish is a crisp, crunchy, round root vegetable with leafy tops that grows under the soil.

Example

I don't like the taste of radish, it's kind of yucky!

무, 래디시 : 흙 속에서 자라는 둥글고 아삭아삭한 붉은 뿌리 채소입니다.

hurt

verb

Meaning

to have pain

In simple words

To hurt means to feel sore pain when you scrape or bump your body.

Example

I hurt my hand yesterday when I fell on the ground.

다치다, 아프다 : 넘어지거나 부딪혀서 손이나 무릎이 욱신거리고 아픈 상태입니다.

decide

verb

Meaning

to choose something

In simple words

To decide means to think carefully and make a final choice between options.

Example

Let's decide what movie to watch tonight.

결정하다, 고르다 : 여러 영화 포스터를 보며 고민 끝에 '이걸로 봐야지!' 하고 최종 선택을 내리는 동작입니다.

liar

noun

Meaning

someone who tells lies

In simple words

A liar is a person who often tells fibs and does not speak the truth.

Example

Nobody trusted the old lady because she was a famous liar.

거짓말쟁이 : 사실이 아닌 거짓말을 자꾸 해서 다른 사람들의 신뢰를 잃어버린 사람을 말합니다.

attach

verb

Meaning

to connect something

In simple words

To attach means to clip, fasten, or join one thing onto another so they stay connected.

Example

I attached the leash to the dog's collar and took it for a walk.

붙이다, 연결하다 : 산책을 나가기 위해 강아지 목줄 고리를 목걸이 링에 딸깍 하고 연결하는 동작입니다.

interest

noun

Meaning

something you enjoy doing or want to learn about

In simple words

An interest is a fun hobby or topic that you love doing and want to learn all about!

Example

Reading books is my biggest interest, it's my favorite hobby!

관심사, 흥미 : 자기가 정말 재미있어하고 더 배우고 싶어 하는 가장 좋아하는 취미나 활동입니다.
