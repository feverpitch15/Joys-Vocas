Nonfiction Vocabulary

November Week 2 · Study for Test in Week 3 · Explain each word in your own English.

홈으로

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

respond

verb

Meaning

to do something after something has happened

In simple words

To respond means to answer someone or react after something happens.

Example

The boy was sleeping and didn't respond when I said his name.

대답하다, 반응하다 : 어떤 일이 일어난 후 대답하거나 반응을 보이는 행동입니다.

shipwreck

noun

Meaning

a sunken and broken ship or boat

In simple words

A shipwreck is an old broken ship that sank down to the ocean floor.

Example

The island was famous for its shipwrecks. People still explore them today.

난파선 : 바다에 가라앉아 부서진 배를 말합니다. 난파선 주변에는 산호와 물고기가 모여들기도 해요.

confess

verb

Meaning

to tell the truth

In simple words

To confess means to tell the truth about something you did wrong.

Example

The thief confessed that he stole the handbag.

자백하다, 고백하다 : 숨기지 않고 솔직하게 진실이나 잘못을 털어놓는 것입니다.

lately

adverb

Meaning

recently, these days

In simple words

Lately means in recent days or these days, doing something often.

Example

Lately, I've been really interested in cooking. I cook every day!

요즘, 최근에 : '요즘 들어서', '최근에'라는 뜻으로 요즘 매일 하고 있는 일을 말할 때 쓰입니다.

circus

noun

Meaning

a group of clowns, acrobats and animals that perform funny shows

In simple words

A circus is a fun show in a big tent with clowns, acrobats, and performers.

Example

I hate circuses because they always have scary clowns.

서커스 : 광대, 곡예사, 동물들이 재미있는 재주와 공연을 보여주는 큰 천막 쇼입니다.

mystery

noun

Meaning

something difficult to explain or understand

In simple words

A mystery is a secret puzzle that is hard to explain or understand.

Example

The detective worked hard to solve the mystery of the missing painting.

수수께끼, 미스터리 : 이유를 알기 어렵거나 설명하기 힘든 신비롭고 풀기 어려운 사건이나 비밀입니다.

crash

verb

Meaning

to hit into something hard

In simple words

To crash means to bump or smash hard into something with a loud hit.

Example

The old lady crashed her car into the bridge, because of ice on the road.

충돌하다, 부딪히다 : 무언가 단단한 것에 쾅 하고 세게 부딪히는 것입니다.

regular

adjective

Meaning

normal, usual

In simple words

Regular means normal, ordinary, and usual—an everyday thing.

Example

My day was just regular. Nothing special happened.

보통의, 평범한 : 특별하지 않고 늘 그렇듯 일상적이고 평범한 상태를 뜻합니다.

clatter

noun

Meaning

a loud banging noise

In simple words

A clatter is a loud rattling or banging noise of things falling.

Example

The boxes made a loud clatter as they fell on the ground.

달그락(덜거덕)거리는 큰 소리 : 딱딱한 물건이나 상자들이 우르르 바닥에 떨어질 때 나는 요란한 소리입니다.

jerk

verb

Meaning

to grab and pull something suddenly

In simple words

To jerk means to quickly grab and pull something with a sharp motion.

Example

The door was stuck, so I jerked it open.

홱 잡아당기다 : 멈춰있거나 꽉 끼인 물건을 갑작스럽게 힘을 주어 홱 당기는 동작입니다.
