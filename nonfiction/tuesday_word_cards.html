Nonfiction Vocabulary

Unit 4-6 · Vocabulary Practice · Explain each word in your own English.

홈으로

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

rusty

adjective

Meaning

when metal is old and covered in brown dust

In simple words

Rusty means old metal that has turned reddish-brown and squeaky from being left in the rain or air.

Example

The old bicycle in the garage had a rusty chain and squeaky wheels.

녹슨 : 비를 맞거나 오래되어 쇠붙이에 붉은 갈색 녹이 슬어 삐걱거리는 상태입니다.

accurate

adjective

Meaning

correct in all details; exact

In simple words

Accurate means completely correct, exact, and without any mistakes at all.

Example

The scientist's measurements were accurate and carefully done.

정확한, 정밀한 : 과녁 정중앙에 화살이 꽂히듯, 한 치의 오차나 틀림없이 꼭 들어맞는 상태입니다.

deadly

adjective

Meaning

causing or able to cause death

In simple words

Deadly describes something so dangerous or poisonous that it can cause death.

Example

The spider's venom is deadly to its prey, quickly turning them into dinner!

치명적인, 치사의 : 독거미나 독사처럼 먹잇감의 목숨을 앗아갈 수 있을 정도로 매우 위험하고 강력한 독성을 가진 상태입니다.

gasp

verb

Meaning

to show surprise

In simple words

To gasp means to take a quick, sharp breath in surprise with your mouth wide open!

Example

My mom gasped with shock when she opened her surprise present.

(놀라서) 헉 하고 숨을 들이쉬다 : 깜짝 선물을 열었을 때 두 손으로 입을 가리고 '헉!' 숨을 들이켜는 동작입니다.

nocturnal

adjective

Meaning

active at night

In simple words

Nocturnal describes animals like owls that sleep in the daytime and wake up active at night.

Example

Owls are known for being nocturnal creatures, active mostly during the night.

야행성의, 밤에 활동하는 : 부엉이나 박쥐처럼 낮에는 자고 캄캄한 밤이 되면 깨어나 활동하는 특성입니다.

hide

verb

Meaning

put or keep out of sight

In simple words

To hide means to put something or yourself in a secret place where nobody can see it.

Example

She decided to hide her secret diary under her bed. She hoped no one would find it there.

숨기다, 감추다 : 다른 사람 눈에 띄지 않도록 비밀 일기장을 침대 밑 깊숙이 쏙 밀어 넣어 감추는 행동입니다.

wisdom

noun

Meaning

good ideas and smart thoughts

In simple words

Wisdom means having great knowledge and good judgment to make smart, kind choices.

Example

His years of experience of ruling his country gave the king great wisdom, which he used to rule wisely and kindly.

지혜, 현명함 : 수많은 경험을 통해 얻은 깊은 생각과 현명한 판단력으로 올바른 결정을 내리는 힘입니다.

nephew

noun

Meaning

a son of one's brother or sister

In simple words

A nephew is the son of your brother or sister.

Example

My little nephew loves playing soccer with his friends every weekend. I always try to cheer him on because he's family.

조카 (남자 조카) : 형제나 자매의 아들을 뜻하며, 축구를 좋아하는 귀여운 남자 조카를 응원하는 가족 관계입니다.

chase

verb

Meaning

to run after someone or something

In simple words

To chase means to run quickly after someone or a bouncing ball to catch it.

Example

The dog loves to chase after the ball in the park during our evening walks.

뒤쫓다, 쫓아가다 : 공원 잔디밭 위로 튀어가는 공을 잡으려고 와다다 빠르게 뒤쫓아 달려가는 동작입니다.

real

adjective

Meaning

something that exists

In simple words

Real means truly existing, actual, and not fake or imaginary.

Example

The archeologist who discovered the lost jewels, had to test them to make sure that they were real.

진짜의, 실제의 : 가짜나 장난감이 아니라, 실제로 존재하는 진짜 보석이나 물건임을 뜻합니다.
