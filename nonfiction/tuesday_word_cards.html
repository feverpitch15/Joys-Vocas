Nonfiction Vocabulary

Week 3 (Week 4 Test) · Vocabulary Cards · Explain each word in your own English.

홈으로

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

head for

phrasal verb

Meaning

to go towards

In simple words

To head for somewhere means to start moving or walking towards that place.

Example

Jane headed for the door. She wanted to go home.

~를 향해 가다 : 문이나 집처럼 가고자 하는 목적지를 향해 발걸음을 옮겨 나아가는 모습입니다.

simple

adjective

Meaning

being easy, not difficult

In simple words

Simple means very easy to understand, do, or learn, without anything hard.

Example

The game was really simple and easy to learn. We all had a lot of fun with it.

간단한, 쉬운 : 규칙이 복잡하지 않고 누구나 금방 배워서 즐겁게 할 수 있을 만큼 쉬운 상태입니다.

preen

verb

Meaning

to clean feathers (used for birds)

In simple words

To preen means for a bird to clean and smooth its feathers with its beak.

Example

The parrot preened its feathers with its beak.

(새가) 깃털을 다듬다 : 새가 부리로 깃털에 묻은 먼지를 털어내고 매끄럽고 윤기 나게 가다듬는 예쁜 행동입니다.

company

noun

Meaning

a business

In simple words

A company is a business where people work together to make things or help customers.

Example

My dad works for a big company called Samsung.

회사, 기업 : 사람들이 함께 모여 유용한 제품을 만들거나 서비스를 제공하며 일하는 일터입니다.

slowpoke

noun

Meaning

used to describe someone who is slow

In simple words

A slowpoke is a playful name for someone or something that moves very slowly.

Example

Come on slowpoke! We're going to be late!

느림보, 거북이 : 행동이나 걸음걸이가 느릿느릿해서 다른 사람보다 늦어지는 친구를 친근하게 부르는 말입니다.

navigate

verb

Meaning

to find your way

In simple words

To navigate means to find your way and follow the correct path without getting lost.

Example

We couldn't navigate our way out of the maze. We were lost!

길을 찾아 나아가다 : 미로나 복잡한 길에서 지도를 보며 방향을 잃지 않고 길을 찾아 헤쳐 나가는 것입니다.

useful

adjective

Meaning

having many uses

In simple words

Useful means helpful, practical, and having many good uses to solve problems.

Example

I read a really useful book about how to get rich in twenty days.

유용한, 쓸모 있는 : 많은 도움을 주고 다방면으로 쓸 데가 많아 유익한 상태입니다.

charming

adjective

Meaning

pleasant, polite, likable

In simple words

Charming means polite, sweet, and friendly so that everybody likes and admires you.

Example

The handsome prince was charming and loved by everybody.

매력적인, 호감 가는 : 다정하고 예의 바르며 주변 사람들을 기분 좋게 만들어 모두에게 사랑받는 모습입니다.

ancient

adjective

Meaning

being from a long time ago

In simple words

Ancient means from thousands of years ago in very old history.

Example

The ancient Egyptians built the pyramids.

고대의, 아주 먼 옛날의 : 피라미드처럼 수천 년 전 역사 속 머나먼 과거의 시절이나 유적을 뜻합니다.

the early days

phrase

Meaning

the beginning of something

In simple words

The early days means the very first time when something started long ago.

Example

In the early days, nobody lived on the island. It was empty.

초창기, 초기 시절 : 어떤 일이나 장소가 처음 시작되거나 만들어지던 무렵의 초기를 의미합니다.
