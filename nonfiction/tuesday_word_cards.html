Nonfiction Vocabulary

Unit 4-5 · Vocabulary Practice · Explain each word in your own English.

홈으로

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

shaped

adjective

Meaning

formed; the way something looks

In simple words

Shaped describes having a particular form or appearance, like a heart or a star.

Example

The cookie cutter made heart-shaped cookies for the Valentine's Day party.

~모양의, 형성된 : 하트 모양 틀로 꾹 찍어낸 쿠키처럼, 특정한 모양을 띠고 있는 상태를 말합니다.

simple

adjective

Meaning

easily understood or done; not hard

In simple words

Simple means very easy to draw, do, or understand without being difficult.

Example

She drew a simple stick-man on the paper, it was really easy to draw.

간단한, 단순한 : 복잡하지 않고 동그라미와 직선 몇 개로 졸라맨(스틱맨)을 쉽게 그리듯 아주 쉬운 상태입니다.

centimeter

noun

Meaning

1/100th of a meter

In simple words

A centimeter is a small length unit on a ruler used to measure how long pencils or small items are.

Example

The ruler measured the pencil to be ten centimeters long.

센티미터 (cm) : 자에 적혀 있는 작은 눈금 단위로, 연필이나 지우개처럼 작은 물건의 길이를 잴 때 쓰는 단위입니다.

tube

noun

Meaning

a long, hollow cylinder

In simple words

A tube is a long round hollow container that you can squeeze toothpaste or paint out of.

Example

He squeezed toothpaste from the tube onto his toothbrush.

튜브, 통 : 치약이나 물감처럼 손으로 꾹 눌러서 내용물을 짜낼 수 있는 긴 원통형 용기입니다.

vitamin

noun

Meaning

things found in food that keep you healthy

In simple words

Vitamins are healthy nutrients in fruits and vegetables that give you energy and keep your body strong.

Example

She takes vitamins daily to ensure she gets healthy nutrients.

비타민 : 오렌지, 사과, 채소 등에 들어 있어 우리 몸을 튼튼하고 건강하게 지켜주는 소중한 영양소입니다.

mall

noun

Meaning

a building with lots of stores

In simple words

A mall is a big indoor shopping center filled with clothes shops, toy stores, and restaurants.

Example

We visited the mall to shop for new clothes and enjoy a meal at the food court.

쇼핑몰 : 한 건물 안에 옷가게, 장난감점, 푸드코트 등 다양한 상점들이 모여 있는 대형 쇼핑 공간입니다.

wear

verb

Meaning

to have clothes on your body

In simple words

To wear means to put clothes, shoes, or a hat onto your body.

Example

She couldn't decide what to wear to the party, so she tried on several dresses before choosing one.

(옷 등을) 입다, 착용하다 : 거울 앞에서 파티에 어울리는 예쁜 드레스를 직접 몸에 걸쳐 입어보는 동작입니다.

fury

noun

Meaning

anger

In simple words

Fury means extreme, blazing anger where someone feels very mad and might shout.

Example

My brother exploded with fury when he realized I broke his favorite toy. He shouted and screamed at me!

격분, 엄청난 분노 : 소중한 장난감이 부서졌을 때처럼, 얼굴이 빨개질 정도로 몹시 화가 나서 소리치는 상태를 뜻합니다.

empty

adjective

Meaning

having nothing inside

In simple words

Empty means completely without anything inside, with all contents gone.

Example

After finishing the last slice of pizza, the box was left empty on the table.

텅 빈, 비어 있는 : 피자 마지막 조각까지 맛있게 다 먹고 나서 상자 안에 아무것도 남아있지 않은 상태입니다.

villain

noun

Meaning

a bad person

In simple words

A villain is a bad, evil character in stories and comic books who causes trouble for heroes.

Example

The hero fought the evil villain bravely and saved the city!

악당 : 만화나 동화 속에서 도시를 어지럽히고 영웅과 맞서 싸우는 나쁜 캐릭터입니다.
