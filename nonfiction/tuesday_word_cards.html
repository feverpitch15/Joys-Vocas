Nonfiction Vocabulary

Review Test · Selected Vocabulary Review · Explain each word in your own English.

홈으로

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

paradise

noun

Meaning

a beautiful, perfect place

In simple words

A paradise is a wonderful, peaceful place where everything is beautiful and perfect.

Example

The beautiful island was a paradise with its white sandy beaches and incredible, blue water.

낙원, 파라다이스 : 해먹에 누워 주스를 마시고 돌고래가 뛰노는 것처럼 아름답고 완벽한 최고의 장소입니다.

scrub

verb

Meaning

to roughly clean something

In simple words

To scrub means to rub something really hard with a brush or sponge to make it clean.

Example

He had to scrub the stubborn stain on the carpet multiple times to remove it completely.

문질러 씻다 (닦다) : 더러운 얼룩이나 때를 솔이나 수세미로 힘주어 쓱쓱 문질러 닦아내는 동작입니다.

gobble

verb

Meaning

to eat quickly like an animal

In simple words

To gobble means to eat food very quickly and hungrily, like an eager animal!

Example

The children gobbled up their dinner, excited to have their favorite meal.

게걸스럽게 (허겁지겁) 먹다 : 양손으로 음식을 쥐고 볼이 빵빵해질 정도로 배고픈 동물처럼 허겁지겁 빠르게 먹는 행동입니다.

invent

verb

Meaning

to make something new

In simple words

To invent means to create or design something new that has never existed before.

Example

The scientist worked tirelessly in his lab to invent a new medicine.

발명하다 : 세상에 없던 새로운 기계나 장치, 물건 등을 처음으로 만들어내는 것입니다.

tiny

adjective

Meaning

very small

In simple words

Tiny means very, very small—much smaller than normal!

Example

Aw! Look at that tiny mouse! It's so small and cute!

아주 작은 : 사람의 손가락 끝에 콩알만 하게 쏙 올라갈 정도로 아주 앙증맞고 작은 크기를 말합니다.

peculiar

adjective

Meaning

Strange, unusual.

In simple words

Peculiar means unusual, strange, or quirky in a special, whimsical way.

Example

The old house had a peculiar charm, with its crooked windows and overgrown garden.

특이한, 기묘한 : 줄무늬 대신 알록달록 땡땡이 무늬를 뽐내는 얼룩말처럼 보통과 다르게 신기하고 독특한 상태입니다.

lucky

adjective

Meaning

Having good things happen.

In simple words

Lucky means having good things happen or experiencing great fortune.

Example

Jenny was lucky to find her missing ring in the cafe several days later.

운이 좋은, 행운의 : 뜻밖의 좋은 일이 생기거나 행복한 행운을 얻은 상태입니다.

sail

verb

Meaning

To travel in a boat.

In simple words

To sail means to travel across the water on a boat with the wind.

Example

They got onto the boat, ready to sail away to distant islands.

항해하다, 돛을 달고 나아가다 : 바람을 타고 배를 몰아 바다나 호수를 건너가는 동작입니다.

shell

noun

Meaning

The hard outer case of animals like crabs or snails.

In simple words

A shell is the hard protective outer cover that keeps snails and crabs safe.

Example

We walked along the beach, picking up shells and pretty rocks.

껍질, 조개껍데기 : 조개, 달팽이, 게 등의 몸을 단단하게 감싸 보호하는 겉껍데기입니다.

ability

noun

Meaning

A skill or power.

In simple words

Ability is the special skill or power you have to do something really well.

Example

Her ability to learn new languages quickly impressed everyone.

능력, 재능 : 어떤 일을 훌륭하게 해낼 수 있는 특별한 재능이나 힘입니다.
