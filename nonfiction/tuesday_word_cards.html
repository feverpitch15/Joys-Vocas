Nonfiction Vocabulary

Unit 4-8 · Vocabulary Cards · Explain each word in your own English.

홈으로

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

in tears

idiom

Meaning

crying; weeping

In simple words

To be in tears means having real tears roll down your cheeks because you feel sad or touched.

Example

I was in tears when I learned that my best friend was leaving the country. I'd miss her so much!

눈물을 흘리는, 울고 있는 : 단짝 친구가 멀리 떠나게 되어 너무 슬프고 아쉬운 마음에 두 눈에서 눈물이 주르륵 흐르는 상태입니다.

tough

adjective

Meaning

difficult; hard to do

In simple words

Tough means very hard and challenging to do or solve.

Example

That test was really tough! I didn't know how to answer most of the questions.

힘든, 어려운, 만만치 않은 : 문제가 너무 어려워 머리를 긁적이며 고민할 정도로 쉽게 풀리지 않는 상태입니다.

sturdy

adjective

Meaning

strongly and solidly built

In simple words

Sturdy describes something built so strong and solid that it does not wobble or break easily.

Example

The new bridge over the river was strong and sturdy. It needed to carry lots of weight.

튼튼한, 견고한 : 거대하고 무거운 코끼리가 성큼성큼 건너가도 금 하나 안 가고 흔들림 없이 끄떡없이 버텨내는 튼튼한 상태입니다.

sickly

adjective

Meaning

weak and often ill

In simple words

Sickly describes someone or a creature that feels weak, unhealthy, and needs gentle care.

Example

The baby duck was sickly and needed to be cared for. Gemma nursed it back to health.

병약한, 자주 아픈 : 기운이 없어 축 늘어진 아기 오리처럼, 몸이 허약하여 따뜻한 간호와 정성 어린 보살핌이 필요한 상태입니다.

hut

noun

Meaning

a small, simple house or shelter

In simple words

A hut is a small, cozy shelter built simply out of wood or thatch.

Example

The witch lived in a small, wooden hut in the middle of the forest.

오두막, 작은 집 : 깊은 숲속에 통나무와 짚으로 소박하게 지어 굴뚝에서 연기가 피어오르는 작고 아담한 쉼터입니다.

improve

verb

Meaning

to get better at something

In simple words

To improve means to practice and grow better and better at doing something!

Example

I want to improve my reading skills, so I'm going to practice every night before bed.

향상되다, 나아지다 : 매일 밤 책 읽기를 열심히 연습해서 어제보다 실력이 쑥쑥 늘고 레벨업하는 것입니다.

furious

adjective

Meaning

extremely angry

In simple words

Furious means super, extraordinarily angry, so angry that you feel like steam is coming out of your ears!

Example

Jamie was furious when his sister broke his phone. I'd never seen him so angry.

몹시 화가 난, 격노한 : 소중한 물건이 망가졌을 때처럼 얼굴이 빨개지고 귀에서 김이 날 정도로 머리끝까지 화가 난 상태입니다.

delicate

adjective

Meaning

easily broken or damaged; fragile

In simple words

Delicate describes something very fragile that needs gentle care so it will not break.

Example

Please be careful with that vase, it's very delicate. If you drop it, it will easily break.

연약한, 깨지기 쉬운 : 얇은 유리 꽃병처럼 조금만 툭 부딪치거나 떨어뜨려도 쉽게 금이 가거나 깨질 수 있어 조심조심 다뤄야 하는 상태입니다.

heap

verb

Meaning

to put in a large pile

In simple words

To heap means to gather and pile a lot of things into a tall, big stack.

Example

I heaped the branches and leaves into a big pile, ready to put in the trash.

수북이 쌓다, 쌓아 올리다 : 가을 마당에서 갈퀴로 낙엽과 나뭇가지들을 모아 산더미처럼 커다랗게 쌓아 올리는 행동입니다.

voice

noun

Meaning

the sound made by a person speaking or singing

In simple words

A voice is the lovely sound made when someone speaks or sings with their mouth.

Example

The singer has a beautiful voice. She's famous for her amazing singing skills.

목소리, 음성 : 무대에서 가수가 마이크를 잡고 노래할 때 입에서 흘러나오는 아름답고 맑은 노랫소리입니다.
