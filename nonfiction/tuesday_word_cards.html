Nonfiction Vocabulary

Unit 4-7 · Vocabulary Cards · Explain each word in your own English.

홈으로

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

thin

adjective

Meaning

not thick; narrow

In simple words

Thin means not thick or fat, like a single delicate sheet of paper.

Example

The old paper was thin and delicate. It was easily ripped.

얇은, 가는 : 두꺼운 백과사전 옆에 아주 얇은 책의 옆면처럼, 두께가 거의 느껴지지 않을 정도로 얇은 상태를 말합니다.

gross

adjective

Meaning

horrible, nasty, disgusting

In simple words

Gross describes something super yucky, nasty, or disgusting that makes you pinch your nose!

Example

The smell of rotten eggs was gross. I felt sick!

역겨운, 징그러운, 고약한 : 썩은 달걀 냄새처럼 고약해서 '으~' 소리가 나고 코를 꽉 쥐게 만드는 불쾌한 상태입니다.

wild

adjective

Meaning

not tame, from nature

In simple words

Wild means living free in nature, not tamed or trained to be a gentle pet.

Example

Be careful of that wild dog. He isn't trained to be friendly to humans!

야생의, 길들여지지 않은 : 집에서 사랑받는 순한 반려견과 달리, 목줄 없이 자연 속에서 스스로 살아가는 길들여지지 않은 동물입니다.

liver

noun

Meaning

part of the body that cleans the blood

In simple words

The liver is an amazing organ inside our body that cleans our blood and takes vitamins out of food.

Example

The liver helps to clean our blood, and takes vitamins out of food.

간 (간장) : 우리 몸속에서 혈액을 맑고 깨끗하게 걸러주고, 음식물에서 소중한 비타민과 영양소를 흡수하도록 돕는 중요한 장기입니다.

toad

noun

Meaning

an animal like a frog

In simple words

A toad is a bumpy creature like a frog that sits by a pond and catches bugs with a fast sticky tongue.

Example

The tiny toad sat on a rock by the pond. It caught flies with its long, sticky tongue.

두꺼비 : 개구리와 비슷하지만 피부가 올록볼록하며, 연못가 바위 위에 앉아 있다가 긴 혀로 곤충을 쏙 잡아먹는 동물입니다.

rainforest

noun

Meaning

a forest with lots of rain

In simple words

A rainforest is a lush, green tropical forest where it rains a lot and many wild jungle animals live.

Example

The rainforest is an interesting place, with lots of animals that live in green jungles.

열대우림 : 거대한 열대 나뭇잎 위로 시원한 비가 쏟아져 내리고, 투칸 등 신기한 동물들이 서식하는 울창한 초록빛 정글 밀림입니다.

shrub

noun

Meaning

a small to medium-sized woody plant

In simple words

A shrub is a bushy, woody garden plant that is smaller than a tree and has many branches.

Example

The gardener trimmed the shrub to give it a neat and tidy appearance in his garden.

관목, 덤불 나무 : 커다란 키다리 나무와 달리, 땅 밑동에서부터 여러 갈래의 가지가 빽빽하게 자라나 낮고 동그랗게 우거진 덤불 식물입니다.

creek

noun

Meaning

a small stream or brook

In simple words

A creek is a clear, shallow stream or little brook of fresh water flowing in nature.

Example

The children played by the creek, skipping stones and searching for fish in the small river.

시내, 냇가, 작은 개울 : 큰 강보다 폭이 좁고 물이 얕아 조약돌 물수제비도 뜨고 작은 물고기도 관찰할 수 있는 맑고 졸졸 흐르는 개울가입니다.

sorts

noun

Meaning

different kinds or types

In simple words

Sorts means many different kinds, varieties, or types of things you can enjoy.

Example

She loved most music and enjoyed all sorts, from K-pop to jazz to rock!

여러 가지 종류 : K-pop, 재즈, 록처럼 한 가지만 있는 것이 아니라 다양하게 나뉘는 여러 '종류'나 '갈래'를 뜻합니다.

promise

verb

Meaning

to say you will do something

In simple words

To promise means to give your word and say for sure that you will do something important.

Example

She promised to always be there for her best friend, no matter what challenges they faced together.

약속하다 : 친구와 손가락을 꼭 걸고 "내가 꼭 그렇게 할게!" 하고 마음을 담아 다짐하는 행동입니다.
