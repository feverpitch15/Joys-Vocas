Nonfiction Vocabulary

Unit 4-3 · Explain each word in your own English.

홈으로

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

cocoon

nounWhat is a cocoon?Think first

Book meaning

A silky shell made by insects when they change shape.

In your own words

A cocoon is a soft, silky home where a caterpillar sleeps and turns into a butterfly.

Example

Inside the cocoon, the caterpillar changes, ready to be a butterfly.

부모 힌트: 나뭇가지에 매달린 번데기 고치 그림을 보며 “애벌레가 나비로 변하기 위해 머무는 실크로 만든 집”이라고 알려 주세요.

shell

nounWhere do shells come from?Think first

Book meaning

The hard outer case of animals like crabs or snails.

In your own words

A shell is the hard outer cover that protects animals like crabs or snails.

Example

We walked along the beach, picking up shells and pretty rocks.

부모 힌트: 모래사장 위의 조개껍데기들을 보며 “소라나 게, 달팽이처럼 부드러운 몸을 감싸주는 딱딱한 껍데기”라고 설명해 주세요.

huge

adjectiveHow big is something that is huge?Think first

Book meaning

Very big.

In your own words

Huge means extremely big, tall, or gigantic!

Example

The huge old oak tree in the park was taller than a house!

부모 힌트: 집보다 훨씬 더 거대한 오크나무를 보며 “그냥 큰(big) 것을 넘어 엄청나게 거대한 것”을 말할 때 쓴다고 짚어 주세요.

aboard

adverbWhat does it mean to be aboard?Think first

Book meaning

On or in a ship, plane, or train.

In your own words

Aboard means being on a ship, plane, or train for a trip.

Example

The captain welcomed everyone aboard the ship, ready for an exciting journey.

부모 힌트: 배를 타는 승객과 선장님을 보며 “배나 기차, 비행기에 올라탄(탑승한) 상태”라고 설명해 주세요. (“All aboard!”)

mostly

adverbWhen do we use mostly?Think first

Book meaning

Almost; nearly.

In your own words

Mostly means almost all the time, or nearly all of something.

Example

She mostly prefers to read history books, but sometimes enjoys fiction.

부모 힌트: 책탑에 둘러싸여 역사책을 읽는 모습을 보며 “대부분, 거의 다”라는 뜻으로 평소 주로 하는 것을 말할 때 쓴다고 알려 주세요.

ability

nounWhat is an ability?Think first

Book meaning

A skill or power.

In your own words

Ability is the special skill or power you have to do something well.

Example

Her ability to learn new languages quickly impressed everyone.

부모 힌트: 음악과 외국어 재능을 뽐내는 모습을 보며 “무언가를 잘해내는 능력, 솜씨나 재능”이라고 설명해 주세요.

tense

adjectiveHow do you feel when you are tense?Think first

Book meaning

Not relaxed; hard and tight.

In your own words

Tense means feeling nervous, worried, or not relaxed.

Example

The feeling in the classroom became tense as they awaited test results.

부모 힌트: 시험지를 기다리며 굳어있는 학생을 보며 “마음이 조마조마하고 온몸이 긴장된 상태”를 뜻한다고 알려 주세요.

realize

verbWhat happens when you realize something?Think first

Book meaning

To suddenly know.

In your own words

To realize means to suddenly understand or remember something.

Example

The boy searched for his keys, before he realized they were in his pocket.

부모 힌트: 머리 위에 전구가 번쩍 켜지는 모습을 보며 “아, 맞다! 하고 문득 깨닫거나 알아차리는 것”이라고 설명해 주세요.

enough

adjectiveIs it too little or enough?Think first

Book meaning

The amount you need.

In your own words

Enough means as much as you need—not too little, just right!

Example

She studied hard and felt confident that she had done enough to pass.

부모 힌트: 공부를 마치고 자신 있게 엄지를 든 모습을 보며 “모자라지 않고 필요한 만큼 충분한” 상태를 말해 주세요.

sparkly

adjectiveWhat makes something sparkly?Think first

Book meaning

Shiny and reflecting light.

In your own words

Sparkly means shining brightly with flashing glimmers of light!

Example

The diamond necklace she wore to the party was sparkly and beautiful.

부모 힌트: 빛을 받아 별처럼 반짝반짝 빛나는 보석 목걸이를 보며 “반짝거리고 눈부시게 빛나는 모양”을 짚어 주세요.
