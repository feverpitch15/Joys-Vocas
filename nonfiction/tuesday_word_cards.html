Nonfiction Vocabulary

Review Test · 10 Selected Vocabulary Review · Explain each word in your own English.

홈으로

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

worth

noun

Meaning

how important or expensive something is

In simple words

Worth means how valuable, precious, or expensive something is, like shiny gold or a diamond.

Example

That diamond is worth a lot of money.

가치, 값어치 : 반짝이는 보석처럼 어떤 물건이 얼마나 소중하고 비싼지를 나타내는 값어치입니다.

lagoon

noun

Meaning

a pool of sea water

In simple words

A lagoon is a calm, shallow pool of clear blue sea water separated from the deeper ocean by a reef or sandbar.

Example

When it rains a lot, the lagoon fills up with water.

라군, 석호 (바닷물 웅덩이) : 바다와 모래톱으로 나뉘어 잔잔하고 에메랄드빛으로 고여 있는 맑은 바닷물 웅덩이입니다.

envy

verb

Meaning

to want something somebody else has

In simple words

To envy means to wish that you had something cool, special, or nice that another person has.

Example

My mom envies her sister, because she has a bigger house.

부러워하다, 시샘하다 : 다른 사람이 가진 멋진 물건이나 큰 집을 보고 '나도 저거 갖고 싶다' 하고 부러워하는 마음입니다.

crash

verb

Meaning

to hit into something hard

In simple words

To crash means to slam, bump, or hit into something very hard with a loud noise!

Example

The old lady crashed her car into the bridge, because of ice on the road.

충돌하다, 부딪치다 : 미끄러지거나 달려가다 벽이나 다리에 쿵 하고 세게 부딪치는 동작입니다.

shipwreck

noun

Meaning

a sunken and broken ship or boat

In simple words

A shipwreck is an old broken sailing ship that has sunk deep under the sea.

Example

The island was famous for its shipwrecks. People still explore them today.

난파선, 침몰선 : 바다 깊은 곳에 가라앉아 물고기들의 쉼터가 된 오래된 부서진 배입니다.

clatter

noun

Meaning

a loud banging noise

In simple words

A clatter is a loud, noisy rattling sound made when hard things bang or drop together.

Example

The boxes made a loud clatter as they fell on the ground.

달그락·덜거덕거리는 소리 : 상자들이나 그릇이 바닥에 와르르 쏟아지며 요란하게 부딪히는 소리입니다.

circus

noun

Meaning

a group of clowns, acrobats and animals that perform funny shows

In simple words

A circus is a fun show inside a big striped tent with clowns, acrobats, and funny tricks!

Example

I hate circuses because they always have scary clowns.

서커스 : 커다란 천막 안에서 광대와 곡예사들이 신기하고 재미있는 묘기를 펼치는 쇼입니다.

navigate

verb

Meaning

to find your way

In simple words

To navigate means to find your path and follow the right way without getting lost.

Example

We couldn't navigate our way out of the maze. We were lost!

길을 찾아 나아가다 : 나침반과 지도를 보며 미로나 복잡한 길에서 출구를 찾아 헤쳐 나가는 것입니다.

preen

verb

Meaning

to clean feathers (used for birds)

In simple words

To preen means for a bird to smooth and clean its feathers neatly with its beak.

Example

The parrot preened its feathers with its beak.

(새가) 깃털을 다듬다 : 새가 부리로 깃털을 쓱쓱 빗어 먼지를 털고 매끄럽게 가다듬는 행동입니다.

ancient

adjective

Meaning

being from a long time ago

In simple words

Ancient means belonging to thousands of years ago in very old history.

Example

The ancient Egyptians built the pyramids.

고대의, 아주 먼 옛날의 : 피라미드처럼 수천 년 전 역사 속 머나먼 과거의 시절이나 유적을 뜻합니다.
