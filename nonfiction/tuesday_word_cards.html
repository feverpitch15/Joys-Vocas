Nonfiction Vocabulary

Unit 4-2 · Explain each word in your own English.

홈으로

품사와 교과서 뜻을 확인하고, 쉬운 영어로 뜻을 설명해 보세요.

fantastic

adjectiveWhat does fantastic mean?Think first

Book meaning

Very good, great.

In your own words

Fantastic means really wonderful, great, and exciting!

Example

The amazing performance at the concert last night was absolutely fantastic!

부모 힌트: 환호하는 관객과 멋진 무대 그림을 보며 “정말 최고로 멋지고 훌륭해!” 할 때 쓰는 감탄 표현이라고 알려 주세요.

fairy

nounWhat is a fairy?Think first

Book meaning

A small magical creature with wings.

In your own words

A fairy is a cute little magical person with wings.

Example

The children were delighted to discover a magical fairy hiding among the flowers in the garden.

부모 힌트: 날개와 요술봉을 든 작고 신비로운 요정 그림을 가리키며 “자연 속에 사는 날개 달린 요정”을 떠올리게 해 주세요.

tale

nounWhat is a bedtime tale?Think first

Book meaning

An old story.

In your own words

A tale is an exciting old story or fairy tale.

Example

The boy's mother read him a bedtime tale to help him sleep.

부모 힌트: 엄마가 잠자리에서 읽어주는 동화책 그림을 보며 “입에서 입으로 전해 내려오는 옛날이야기나 신기한 이야기”라고 설명해 주세요.

tiny

adjectiveIs it big or tiny?Think first

Book meaning

Very small.

In your own words

Tiny means something that is very, very small and little.

Example

Aw! Look at that tiny mouse! It's so small and cute!

부모 힌트: 도토리 옆에 앉은 앙증맞은 아기 쥐를 보며 “small보다 훨씬 더 작고 앙증맞은 것”을 표현할 때 쓴다고 짚어 주세요.

actually

adverbWhen do we say actually?Think first

Book meaning

Truthfully, in a real way.

In your own words

Actually means really, truthfully, or in real life.

Example

Actually, I did see him at the party last night, even if he said he didn't see me.

부모 힌트: 돋보기로 진실을 찾은 표정을 보며 “사실은~, 실제로 진짜로는~” 하고 오해를 바로잡거나 진실을 말할 때 쓰는 말이에요.

peculiar

adjectiveWhat makes something peculiar?Think first

Book meaning

Strange, unusual.

In your own words

Peculiar means unusual, strange, or quirky in a special way.

Example

The old house had a peculiar charm, with its crooked windows and overgrown garden.

부모 힌트: 삐뚤빼뚤 독특한 오두막집을 보며 “보통과 다르게 특이하고 기묘하지만 독특한 매력이 있는 것”을 연상시켜 주세요.

feathery

adjectiveHow does something feathery feel?Think first

Book meaning

Light and soft, like a feather.

In your own words

Feathery means soft, light, and fluffy just like feathers.

Example

The bird's delicate, feathery wings shone like gems.

부모 힌트: 활짝 펼쳐진 새의 날개 그림을 보며 “새의 깃털처럼 보들보들하고 가벼운 감촉”을 상상하게 해 주세요.

drag

verbHow do you drag something heavy?Think first

Book meaning

To pull or be pulled along the ground.

In your own words

To drag means to pull something heavy along the floor.

Example

They had to drag the heavy suitcases up the stairs because the elevator was broken.

부모 힌트: 무거운 여행용 가방을 바닥에 질질 끌어당기는 모습을 보며 “바닥에 닿은 채로 힘겹게 끌고 가는 동작”이라고 알려 주세요.

lucky

adjectiveWas it good luck?Think first

Book meaning

Having good things happen.

In your own words

Lucky means having good luck or fortunate things happen.

Example

Jenny was lucky to find her ring in the cafe several days later.

부모 힌트: 풀밭에서 네잎클로버를 찾고 활짝 웃는 모습을 보며 “뜻밖의 좋은 일이 생기는 행운, 운이 좋은 것”을 알려 주세요.

sail

verbWhat do you do on a sailboat?Think first

Book meaning

To travel in a boat.

In your own words

To sail means to travel across the water on a boat.

Example

They got onto the boat, ready to sail away to distant islands.

부모 힌트: 푸른 바다 위를 나아가는 돛단배를 보며 “바람을 타고 배로 바다나 호수를 항해하다”라는 뜻임을 짚어 주세요.
